- summary: How closely does fan engagement follow what happens on the field? A student team studied San Francisco 49ers fans during the 2025–2026 NFL season, combining a survey of 63 fans with weekly Google search interest in California and game-by-game team data. Fans' reported satisfaction and their search activity moved in the same direction, and engagement rose and fell with the team's results, pointing to fan engagement that is largely performance-based and driven by star players. One team member received a mini internship opportunity with the San Francisco 49ers.

## Domain knowledge
### Real fans, real games
- text: The team surveyed 63 fans and matched their answers with weekly search interest and with team statistics for every game of the season, including yards, passing and rushing attempts, turnovers, points, and results.

## Research question
- Do fans' reported engagement and their online search behavior tell the same story? The team asked how team performance shapes fan engagement, whether survey answers line up with search activity, and which fan behaviors show up in search trends. Students also analyzed fans' own words: what the team should improve next season and which player stood out most.
