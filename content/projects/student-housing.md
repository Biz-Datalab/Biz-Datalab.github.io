## Domain knowledge
### Real respondents
- text: 74 Pacific students answered an anonymous survey that ran for four weeks: 38 domestic local, 17 domestic non-local, and 19 international students. Each rated twelve housing factors and shared their own experiences.

## Research question
- What matters most to Pacific students when they decide where to live, and does it differ for international, domestic local, and domestic non-local students? Living on campus supports student retention, a stronger campus community, academic performance, and well-being, so knowing these priorities helps the university make campus housing an easy choice. The team asked students to rate twelve housing factors, from safety and affordability to proximity and amenities, and compared the results across the three groups. The SURF project then asked whether these priorities can predict where a student chooses to live; see the SURF presentation, [Understanding UOP Student Housing Decisions: A Machine Learning Approach ↗](https://scholarlycommons.pacific.edu/surf/120/).

- intro: Selected dashboards from the capstone report and a slide from the SURF presentation.

### All respondents
- text: Safety and security is the top factor overall, a natural strength of living on campus.
- image: projects/student-housing/dashboard-overall.png

### International students
- text: Safety and security ranks first (4.58 of 5), followed by affordability and internet and utilities.
- image: projects/student-housing/dashboard-international.png

### Domestic local students
- text: Affordability (4.24), parking (4.21), and safety (4.16) matter most.
- image: projects/student-housing/dashboard-domestic-local.png

### Domestic non-local students
- text: Proximity to campus ranks first (4.53), and about three in four of these respondents live on campus.
- image: projects/student-housing/dashboard-domestic-nonlocal.png

### SURF: predicting housing choices
- text: A machine learning model (k-nearest neighbors) predicted students' housing choices with 75% accuracy, opening the door to personalized housing recommendations and better use of campus resources.
- image: projects/student-housing/surf-ml-slide.jpg
- size: wide

## Student recommendations
- Highlight campus safety and the convenience of living close to classes, offer flexible and affordable housing options, and tailor outreach to what each student group values most.
