How these numbers are calculated

Each cell samples online listings (Blue Nile) at one point in time. Price per carat = price ÷ carat weight. The 95% confidence interval (CI) = mean ± t(0.975, n−1) × standard error, and it describes the average price, not the spread of individual stones. These are asking prices, not transaction prices.
